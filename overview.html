Stay on Simpleview Rebuild path · best to worst case Breakeven zone

Cumulative cash spent over 60 months. The stay line grows steadily at today's $32,015/mo. The rebuild band climbs faster during the build year (operating costs continue while building) then flattens once the new platform is live. The two paths cross between months 26–39 from project start (14–27 months from launch). By year 5, the rebuild path has saved $265K–$615K versus staying put.

Beyond the numbers — what changes for the audience

For the 8M annual visitors

Faster site · trip-planning AI · multilingual Q&A

Native CMS replaces a vendor platform built in 2014. Page speed, mobile UX, and personalization move from constraint to lever.

For partners (hotels, attractions, Connect Pass)

Self-serve listings · faster updates · richer data

Partner CRM rebuilt with file-ingestion AI. Listing freshness moves from weeks to hours. Migration plan published in Phase 2.

For VSL marketing & brand

Owned design system · campaign-velocity CMS

Move from vendor-templated layouts to a VSL-owned design system. Landing pages and campaign microsites ship in days, not sprints.

Migration risks & mitigations

SEO redirects · email list portability · parallel-run

URL map, 301s, sitemap parity, and dual-running CRM during cutover are Phase 2 deliverables. Subscriber list and deliverability reputation transferred before Act On sunset.
